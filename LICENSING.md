The published analysis at hub.pyc.agency is the canonical home of every index
and finding. This repository exists so the figures can be checked rather than
taken on trust: every score is recomputable from the data here.

If you cite a figure, cite a dated snapshot. Scores are corrected when we find
them wrong, and corrections are logged publicly at
https://hub.pyc.agency/indices/changelog/ — superseded datasets are kept
alongside the current one so an earlier citation stays verifiable.
